# Retail — Uganda Context

## Market Overview
- Small retail shops ("dukas") are the backbone of commerce in refugee settlements and trading centers
- Typical stock: household goods, cooking supplies, personal care, phone accessories, airtime
- Mobile money agent services often co-located with retail shops (additional revenue stream)
- Most retailers operate with very thin margins and high turnover
- Competition is primarily on location, trust/relationships, and credit availability

## Benchmarks for Small Retail
- Gross margins: 15-25% on fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), 30-50% on specialty items
- Monthly revenue for a typical settlement shop: UGX 500,000-3,000,000 ($130-$780)
- Inventory turnover: best shops turn inventory every 7-14 days
- Rent in trading centers: UGX 50,000-200,000/month ($13-$52)
- Mobile money float requirements: UGX 200,000-1,000,000 ($52-$260)

## Distribution & Supply Chain
- Wholesale markets: Gulu (closest major hub for northern Uganda), Kampala for specialty items
- Delivery: hired vehicles, boda-boda for small quantities
- Credit from wholesalers: common arrangement, typically 1-2 week terms
- Bulk buying trips to Gulu every 1-2 weeks
- Some NGOs and organizations run mobile wholesale deliveries to settlements

## Common Challenges
- Working capital constraints — can't stock enough variety or quantity
- Customer credit (buying on "tab") creating cash flow gaps — average 30-40% of sales on credit
- Stock-outs due to poor demand forecasting
- Price competition from neighboring shops selling identical products
- Transport costs eating into margins when restocking

## Opportunities
- Product differentiation: stocking unique items not available at other shops
- Mobile money services as consistent, low-risk income (commissions ~1-3% per transaction)
- Bulk-buying cooperatives with other shop owners to negotiate better wholesale prices
- Customer loyalty through consistent quality, fair pricing, and reliable credit terms
- Adding prepared food or drink sales alongside dry goods (higher margins)
- WhatsApp-based ordering for regular customers with delivery via boda-boda
