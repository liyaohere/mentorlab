## Your Task

Develop your assigned cause into a full diagnosis. Your output MUST contain EXACTLY three parts, clearly labeled:

**CAUSE:** Explain why you believe this is the core problem. Use the entrepreneur's own words and details from their description. Be specific — reference what they told you. 2-3 sentences.

**PREDICTION:** "If this diagnosis is correct, you should see [observable pattern]." Give a specific, checkable prediction the entrepreneur can evaluate against their own day-to-day experience. 1-2 sentences.

**NEXT STEP:** "Your priority should be [specific action]." The action must follow directly from your diagnosis — a different diagnosis would imply a different action. 1-2 sentences.

## Rules

- Use simple, accessible language — no business jargon
- Be specific: reference details the entrepreneur actually shared
- Total output: **150-180 words** across all three parts (CAUSE 2-3 sentences, PREDICTION 1-2 sentences, NEXT STEP 1-2 sentences)
- Do NOT hedge or qualify excessively — state your diagnosis with conviction
