# Ideation / Creative Reframing Version (Archived)

**Status**: Removed from main design doc (April 2026). Kept here for reference if needed for advisor discussions.

**Why archived**: The ideation version's theoretical home (Perry-Smith, Rindova & Martins, Doshi & Hauser) does not overlap with the paper's literature (Baer, Kaplan, Nickerson). Its DV (creative novelty) is fundamentally different from formulation comprehensiveness. Including it in the experiment design would dilute focus and require rewriting the entire paper if pilot results favored it.

---

## What AI agents would generate

Competing **opportunity frames** — "what could your business become?"

## Example (Grace, chapati seller in Palabek)

- **One reading of your situation**: "Looking at what you described, your business is fundamentally a food production operation. The path forward is optimizing your product, your supply chain, and your distribution — becoming the most efficient chapati producer in Palabek."
- **A different reading**: "Your real asset isn't chapati — it's the trust your regular customers have in you. Your business is a community hub that happens to sell food. The path forward is building on those relationships — catering, group orders, becoming a gathering point."
- **A third possibility**: "Both of those miss what's most distinctive about your situation. You have access to ingredients across settlement and host community supply chains that others don't. Your business is a supply chain play — the path forward is becoming a middleman or processor, not a direct-to-consumer seller."

## Agent output structure

Each agent's reframe would contain three parts:

1. **Frame** — A reinterpretation of what the business fundamentally is. Not "you should do X" but "your business is actually a Y business."
2. **What this frame opens up** — If the entrepreneur adopted this lens, what new possibilities become visible?
3. **What this frame closes down** — What the entrepreneur would stop investing in.

## Key difference from formulation version

| | Formulation | Ideation |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | Backward-looking: symptom → cause | Forward-looking: current state → alternative identity |
| Agent says | "Your problem is X because Y" | "Your business is actually a Y business" |
| Entrepreneur evaluates by | Checking evidence: "which cause fits my data?" | Checking resonance: "which frame opens the best future?" |
| Disagreement is about | What's wrong | What's possible |
| Cognitive operation | Diagnostic reasoning | Imaginative reframing |
| DV | Formulation comprehensiveness | Creative novelty + idea diversity |

## Adjudication prompt (Condition 3, ideation version)

1. "Which way of seeing your business resonates most with you? Why?"
2. "What is one new idea or direction this frame opens up that you hadn't considered before?"
